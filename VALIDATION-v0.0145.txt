FAMS Region II Unified — v0.0145
Issue-check and account role-dropdown repair

FIXES
- MAIFIP Edit Account role options now include data-office-role="1" for manager, encoder, and admin, and data-medical-role="1" for social_worker. The facility-aware dropdown controller depends on these attributes. Their absence caused all edit-role options to be hidden/disabled, blocking usertype changes and triggering validation errors.
- AICS and MAIFIP APP_VERSION values synchronized to 0.0145. The previous package had AICS at 0.0144 while MAIFIP's app_version.php remained at 0.0141, allowing stale browser asset caches and inconsistent version reporting.
- Added CHANGELOG-v0.0145.txt.
