FAMS REGION II — CHANGELOG v0.0192

STANDARDIZATION / CONCURRENCY HARDENING
- Bound AICS record-revision proposals to the exact record_version observed when the ticket was submitted.
- AICS revision-ticket approval now locks the linked record, rejects stale proposals, and performs a guarded versioned update before resolving the ticket.
- AICS delete-request tickets now persist the source record version; approval refuses to archive a record that has changed since the request was filed.
- Bound MAIFIP revision and delete-request proposals to their source record_version.
- MAIFIP ticket approval and delete approval now re-read the ticket under lock, verify the source record version, and reject stale or legacy proposals that lack a safe base version.
- MAIFIP ticket editing now locks the pending ticket and linked record, refreshes the proposal's base version, and commits the ticket update with its audit event.
- Expanded the permission/source regression suite with ticket version-guard checks.

COMPATIBILITY NOTE
- Pending legacy revision/delete tickets created before v0.0192 may not contain a base record version. They are intentionally blocked from approval rather than risking a stale overwrite or deletion. The requester should edit/re-submit the ticket to refresh its base version, or an administrator should verify the latest record and create a new request. AICS office-reassignment proposals also carry a base version.
- No data tables are renamed and no existing assistance records are rewritten by this release. The existing shared database remains in use.

PACKAGE
- The unrelated MG Tracking Android application remains excluded from the FAMS package.
