FAMS Region II Unified — v0.0208
Database Integrity and Referential Consistency

- Hardened MAIFIP facility/control-number uniqueness migration. Duplicate facility/control-number pairs are detected before DDL; legacy uniqueness protection is retained until the replacement composite unique index is verified.
- Migration completion is no longer cached after a failed or deferred operation; later requests may retry after the underlying issue is corrected.
- Added a shared transactional status-history writer for both workspaces; it refuses writes outside an active transaction and retains separate AICS/MAIFIP history tables.
- Updated workspace log_action adapters to write through the transaction-required audit contract whenever the caller already has an active transaction; standalone informational events retain their existing standalone behavior.
- Added a CLI-only, read-only database integrity auditor for duplicate control numbers/ticket numbers/offline client IDs, orphaned ticket/history/sync references, and foreign-key inventory.
- Archived-as-deleted references are excluded from unresolved orphan counts when fams_deleted_records is available, preserving the approved-deletion archive design.
- Updated AICS and MAIFIP application version markers to 0.0208.
- No AICS or MAIFIP business rules, workspace boundaries, or existing beneficiary/patient records were changed.
