Deletion and Restoration Reconciliation — v0.0201

AICS direct deletion
- POST-only action branch in records.php; CSRF; AICS edit_records capability.
- Record SELECT ... FOR UPDATE followed by can_access_record_scope recheck.
- Non-admin direct delete restricted to LPGMA workspace WAITING entries; finalized/locked records require administrator ticket approval.
- Full row JSON archive with JSON_THROW_ON_ERROR, active row delete, and audit log in fams_shared_transaction.
- Legacy restore action on records.php returns 405; restore mutations are not duplicated there.

AICS ticket-approved deletion
- tickets.php locks pending ticket and linked record, validates _base_record_version, archives full row, updates ticket, deletes record, and audits in the same transaction.
- This remains a distinct authorization path from direct deletion.

MAIFIP direct deletion
- delete_record.php requires POST, CSRF, MAIFIP edit_records capability, role gate, active facility, facility-qualified row lock, and can_edit_existing_record.
- Admins follow the existing administrator policy; non-admin direct deletion remains limited to authorized editable WAITING records.
- Full row archive, facility-qualified delete, and audit log are transactional.

MAIFIP ticket-approved deletion
- ticket_review.php requires ticket review permissions, locks the OPEN ticket and facility-qualified record, validates base record version, archives full payload, retains resolved ticket audit data, deletes the record, and logs the action transactionally.

Restoration
- AICS trash.php and MAIFIP trash.php are administrator-only and require the relevant workspace edit_records capability plus CSRF.
- Each uses a sequence lock shared with its corresponding create/import path, locks the un-restored archive row, checks payload keys against INFORMATION_SCHEMA destination columns, creates a new record with a new control number and current submission date, resets status to WAITING, marks the archive restored exactly once, and writes an audit event in the transaction.
- Original archive rows remain in Trash history. Restore creates a new record; it does not resurrect the old primary key or erase archive history.

Static review is complete for this phase. Authenticated MySQL staging tests remain pending and are listed in VALIDATION-v0.0201.txt.
