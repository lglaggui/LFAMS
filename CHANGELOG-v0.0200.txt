FAMS Region II — v0.0200
Step 3.3: Ticket Lifecycle Enforcement

Scope implemented
- MAIFIP ticket creation now locks the linked facility-qualified record and verifies that the record version captured when the form was loaded is still current before inserting either a revision or delete ticket.
- MAIFIP ticket editing now rejects stale proposals. It verifies the proposal's original base record version against the current facility-qualified record both before rendering and again under transaction locks. Editing no longer silently rebases a stale proposal to a newer record version.
- AICS revision, deletion, and office-reassignment ticket submissions now re-lock the linked record and recheck office scope and the captured record version before ticket insertion.
- AICS approved revision tickets now write aics_status_history when the proposed status changes, in the same transaction as the record update and ticket decision.
- AICS ticket deletion is restricted to pending, non-record-linked support tickets. Reviewed tickets and record-linked workflow tickets are retained for audit and recovery.
- AICS approval/rejection remains administrator-only, locks the pending ticket, and rejects replayed decisions after the ticket leaves its pending state.
- Updated the endpoint registry with the ticket lifecycle source-review notes. Runtime verification remains explicitly pending.
- Version markers updated to 0.0200.

Not included
- Ticket-driven status enforcement has not been declared complete for all legacy paths.
- Spreadsheet import and offline synchronization enforcement remain separate future phases.
- No database schema migration or production data change was performed in this package.
