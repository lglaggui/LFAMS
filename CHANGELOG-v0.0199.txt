Step 3.2: Record Status Change Enforcement

- Hardened AICS status updates with transactional capability and office-scope revalidation, row locking, mandatory positive record-version matching, and finalized-record protection.
- Hardened MAIFIP status updates with in-transaction capability revalidation, facility-qualified row locking, stale-version protection, and finalized-record protection.
- Explicitly reject unsupported HTTP methods on both status endpoints.
- Kept status-history and audit writes atomic with each successful status update.
- Hardened AICS automatic expiry cancellation: expired WAITING records are locked and version-guarded; status history and audit entries are written in the same transaction.
- Updated endpoint registry status for the two status endpoints.
- Added focused static regression tests.

Not included in this phase: status changes performed as part of ticket resolution or spreadsheet imports. Those are handled in their respective Step 3.3 and Step 3.6 enforcement groups; authenticated staging verification remains required.
