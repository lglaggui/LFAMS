FAMS Region II Unified — v0.0217
MAIFIP Signed Fund Movements and Negative Download Reconciliation

MAIFIP fund ledger
- Removed the zero-floor from get_monthly_report_downloaded_balance(). The helper now returns the signed net sum of ordinary fund-download rows, rounded to cents.
- Negative ordinary fund entries are now accepted by the MAIFIP fund entry form and server-side validation. Positive amounts represent additions; negative amounts represent reversals or corrections.
- Renamed the monthly report amount label to NET FUND DOWNLOADS so the displayed figure is not mistaken for gross receipts.
- Updated the Fund Management labels and guidance to describe signed fund movements.
- Kept the existing DECIMAL(14,2) database column; it is signed by default, so no schema migration is required.
- Preserved the shared monthly reconciliation formula: opening balance + signed net fund downloads + signed adjustments - released assistance.
- The worksheet, print view, and CSV export continue to consume the same authoritative reconciliation result.

Validation
- Added contract checks for signed net totals, negative entry acceptance, and net-download labels.
- Updated release-version assertions to 0.0217.
- No AICS business logic or fund calculation was changed.
- No database migration or production database operation was performed.
