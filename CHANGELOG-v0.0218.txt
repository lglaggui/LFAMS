FAMS Region II Unified — v0.0218
MAIFIP Monthly Reconciliation Consistency and Historical Summary Disclosure

MAIFIP ledger integrity
- Opening-balance uniqueness is now checked across the entire 2026+ facility ledger, not only through the selected reporting period. A later-dated duplicate can no longer remain hidden when an earlier month is viewed.
- The invariant remains: at most one 2026+ BEGINNING BALANCE row per facility, and if one exists it must be dated January 1, 2026.

Monthly detail consistency
- Added one shared MAIFIP released-record retrieval helper for the worksheet, print report, and CSV export.
- Facility and status comparisons are normalized consistently with the authoritative monthly summary helper.
- Replaced YEAR()/MONTH() filtering in the detail views with a half-open release-date range, preserving facility scope and chronological ordering.

Historical summary reconciliation
- The monthly summary helper now retains stored-summary amount/client-count metadata when live RELEASED records exist.
- A saved monthly summary that differs from live RELEASED records is explicitly flagged; live record-level releases remain authoritative for the monthly calculation.
- The worksheet, print report, Excel export, and CSV export disclose stored-summary differences.
- Print wording now distinguishes detail rows matching the selected report total from matching a stored historical summary.

Preserved behavior
- Signed fund downloads and signed adjustments continue to carry into later months.
- Opening balance + signed net fund downloads + signed adjustments - released assistance remains the ending-balance formula.
- AICS business logic and calculations were not changed.
- No database migration was introduced and no production database operation was performed.
